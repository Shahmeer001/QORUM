# QUORUM - Final Presentation Outline

## Slide 1: Title Slide
- **Title:** QUORUM: Cross-Market Multi-Agent Financial Decision Architecture
- **Subtitle:** Supervised Autonomous Debate with Risk Governance

## Slide 2: Problem Statement
- Isolated asset analysis ignores global macro contagion and liquidity spillovers.
- Large language models without structured rules hallucinate and lack quantitative discipline.
- Black-box algorithms fail to provide auditable reasoning for trade recommendations.

## Slide 3: The QUORUM Solution
- 5 Specialized Market Agents: PSX, US, FOREX, GOLD, CRYPTO.
- Constrained Blackboard Architecture: Peer debate with typed messages and strict visibility limits.
- Independent Supervisor & Risk Gatekeeper: Mathematically sound consensus backed by hard veto rules.

## Slide 4: Empirical Research Foundations
- 1-Year 5-minute BTC data analysis (40,000 baseline trades).
- Empirical 3-candle & 7-candle movement patterns (`U-S-D`).
- Exported pattern knowledge directly embedded into agent reasoning engines.

## Slide 5: Experimental Comparison: Single vs Silent vs Debate
- **Single Agent:** Susceptible to local market noise.
- **Silent Consensus:** Simple averaging without challenge.
- **Debate Mode:** Cross-examines conflicting indicators, avoiding high-risk traps and improving Sharpe ratio.

## Slide 6: Live Demonstration & UI Showcase
- New Decision Session configuration.
- Real-time round-by-round debate trace playback.
- Simulation & verified equity curves.
